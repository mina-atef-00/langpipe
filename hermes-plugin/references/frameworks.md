# Level frameworks and exam specifications (Exam-track preset)

Basis for the Exam-track preset (official outline + past-paper
practice) and for the skill's rule: level frameworks are CEFR for
European languages, HSK for Chinese, JLPT for Japanese, and
word-count-per-level targets carry their source.

## Official sources (verify links before sending; record version used)

- CEFR — Council of Europe, *Common European Framework of Reference
  for Languages*. Start at coe.int (search "CEFR"); the Companion
  Volume (2020) extends the 2001 scales. Applies to Spanish (DELE),
  French (DELF/DALF), and other European languages.
- HSK — Chinese Testing International (chinesetest.cn). Note the
  version split: HSK 2.0 (6 levels, long the de-facto standard) vs
  HSK 3.0 (9 levels / bands, rolling out since 2021). Chinese plans
  cite HSK levels rather than inventing milestones — and state which
  HSK version. HSK vocabulary lists are published per level.
- JLPT — Japan Foundation / JEES, jlpt.jp/e/. Five levels N5 (basic)
  to N1 (advanced). No official vocabulary or grammar list is
  published: any third-party "N5 vocab" compilation is `UNVERIFIED`
  unless its source corpus is stated.
- DELE — Instituto Cervantes (cervantes.es), six levels A1-C2
  aligned to the CEFR. Syllabus in the *Plan curricular del
  Instituto Cervantes*.
- DELF/DALF — France Education international
  (france-education-international.fr), A1-C2 aligned to the CEFR.

## Rules for plans

- Exam-track means the board's published specification, not a
  summary of it: link the spec version, list the components
  (reading/listening/writing/speaking, pass thresholds), and build
  past-paper practice around the official format.
- Every word-count-per-level target states its source (exam word
  list version, or Nation 2006 — see `frequency.md`). A target
  without a source is marked `UNVERIFIED`.
- Version-pinning: write "HSK 2.0 Level 3 word list (1,200 words)"
  or "CEFR Companion Volume 2020, B1", never bare "B1" / "HSK 3"
  where the version changes the meaning.
